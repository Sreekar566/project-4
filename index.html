What is a teleconnection? A teleconnection is a climate linkage between two regions that are far apart geographically — when temperatures in one region rise and fall, a connected region tends to follow the same pattern. An edge in this graph means those two regions' temperatures move together across the year. Thicker edges = stronger synchrony. This doesn't mean their climates are identical, just that they're statistically coupled — often driven by shared atmospheric circulation patterns or ocean currents.

Project 4 Prototype Write-Up

What have you done so far?

So far, our prototype is a teleconnection network graph visualizing global temperature teleconnections across 12 biome regions using the NASA MODIS MOD11C3 monthly land surface temperature dataset. In the visualization, each biome is represented by a node. Connecting the nodes are edges where edge thickness is an indicator of the Pearson correlation between two different biomes, signaling how two region’s temperatures rise and fall together across the year. Each of the nodes are also positioned at true latitude/longitude values to preserve geographic information, giving lengths of the edges between each node significance. Interactions include a threshold slider filtering out weaker correlations between biomes and isolating stronger links, giving an option to decrease noise in the data, an animation button that shows how land surface temperatures shift seasonally each month, and a hover interaction that highlights data for a hovered/selected region.

What will be the most challenging part of your project and why?

The most challenging part of the project to design will be the actual story that we want to tell. Redesigning our existing prototype into a story into an experience that is more engaging and personal, rather than just interactive, will be difficult because it requires us to guide the viewer’s experience. This means we pick and choose what the most important takeaway or interesting part of our data/visualization is, requiring a greater understanding of the dataset and what we want our claim to be. Right now, the visualization features a lot of interactive features that allow the viewer to explore the data. A possible complication is that the interactive and explorative aspect of the visualization may show data that contradicts the claim of our story.
